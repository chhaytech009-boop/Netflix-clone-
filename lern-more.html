How to sign in to Netflix

To sign in, follow the steps for your device below.

  • You can sign in with the primary account email or phone number, or an email address added to a secondary profile.
  • If you're having problems signing in to your Netflix account, see Can't sign in to Netflix.

TV or TV streaming device

Sign in on a smart TV or device that connects to a TV, including streaming sticks and media players, cable boxes, Apple TV, and Xbox or PlayStation game consoles.

Use your phone to sign in with a QR code

Open the Netflix app on your TV, select Sign In, and scan the QR code displayed using your phone's camera.

Use a web browser to sign in

Go to netflix.com/tv8 on your computer or mobile device and enter the code displayed on your TV screen.

Use your remote to sign in

Enter your account email and password using your TV remote control and on-screen keyboard.

Phone or tablet

Sign in to the mobile app on an Android phone or tablet, iPhone, or iPad.

Use a sign-in code to sign in

Enter your phone number or email to receive a 4-digit sign-in code sent to your message inbox or email.

Use your password to sign in

Enter your Netflix account email address and account password to sign in directly.

Computer or web browser

Sign in on the Netflix website or the Netflix app for Windows 10 and later.

Use a sign-in code to sign in

Request a sign-in link or code sent to your email or phone number to sign in without typing a password.

Use your password to sign in

Go to netflix.com/login, enter your email/phone number and password, then click Sign In.
